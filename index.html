THE ASSUMPTION AUDIT

The conditions a farm relied on may not be standing still.

Field Shift checks whether recent rainfall, heat exposure, and modeled root-zone wetness still resemble a selected baseline. A deviation starts a field question. It does not prove a crop is unsuitable.

Compare three pathways. Missing field evidence holds a recommendation; a dry-year test reveals water constraints.

METHODOLOGY

What the tool can and cannot say.

The Assumption Audit uses observed NASA POWER daily data from 2016–2025. It compares medians in two five-year windows and selects the observed year with the least growing-season rain as a dry analog. The scenario slider changes only that year's rain total.

It does not predict yield, infer field soil properties, or turn a vegetation image into crop suitability. Farm records and local expertise are needed before changing a rotation.
