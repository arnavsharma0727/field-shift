02 / Research behind the questions

Built from recent NASA work

NASA Harvest · 2025

Sequence changes outcomes

NASA-supported satellite and machine-learning research found that rotation effects varied with crop order and weather. Soybean followed immediately by winter wheat performed poorly in the studied fields, so this prototype flags sequencing as a local check.

Read the NASA research story ↗
NASA DEVELOP · 2024 / archived 2025

Cover crops and water

A northwest Alabama project used Landsat and precipitation observations to study winter cover crops and water retention. It reported higher retention in observed cover-cropped fields while noting satellite resolution and seasonal vegetation limits.

Read the technical report ↗
NASA Acres · 2026

Decisions under water stress

NASA Acres is working with farmers on how Earth observations can support crop, cover, and irrigation decisions amid water constraints. Its Texas High Plains work emphasizes local knowledge and the limits of groundwater.

Read the NASA field story ↗

These projects inform the questions and safeguards in Field Shift. Their satellite products are not all integrated into this version; the live numeric indicators currently come from NASA POWER.

Method & limits. Climate indicators come from NASA POWER daily point data for 2021–2025. The app calculates annual precipitation, hot days, dry spells and monthly rainfall. Strategies use illustrative agronomic heuristics and the farm context you enter; they are not field-validated recommendations, forecasts, or modeled yield estimates. Check planting windows, soil tests, pest history, equipment, seed costs, and local markets with an extension specialist.
