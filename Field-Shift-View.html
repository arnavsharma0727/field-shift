NASA HARVEST / 2025

Sequence is asymmetric.

Satellite and causal machine-learning research found that rotation effects vary by crop order and weather. The app flags soy → winter wheat timing and never transfers study yield percentages to a new field.

HLS PHENOLOGY / 2026

Cover is a time series, not a snapshot.

NASA/USGS Landsat and Sentinel-2 research identifies winter cover by its seasonal fingerprint, but can confuse it with weeds or cash crops. Field dates and soil-cover checks become required evidence here.

NASA ACRES / 2026

Measurement before scale.

NASA Acres measures cover crop growth and termination across thousands of Maryland fields with HLS and field records. This prototype uses a small-trial gate and a baseline comparison rather than a universal recommendation.
