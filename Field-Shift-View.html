08 / METHODS & LIMITATIONS

Reproducible by design

The audit compares medians for 2016–2020 and 2021–2025: seasonal rain (mm), days with maximum air temperature ≥32°C, and modeled root-zone wetness (fraction). The dry analog is the observed complete year with the least seasonal rain. These short windows are descriptive, not a statistical trend or attribution analysis.

Northern seasons are May–September; Southern seasons span November–March and use the ending year. POWER variables are area-scale model or analysis products. The map's MODIS visualization is a separate 2025 context image; pixel values are not extracted and do not enter the audit.
